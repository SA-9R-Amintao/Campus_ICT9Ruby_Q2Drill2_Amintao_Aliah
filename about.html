About Us

OBMC is a learning community committed to helping every student grow in knowledge, character, and confidence. Through caring teachers, a supportive environment, and student-centered learning, we prepare learners to face the future with skill and compassion.

Our Mission

To provide quality, values-driven education that nurtures the intellectual, emotional, social, and moral development of every learner.

Our Vision

To be a trusted school that produces responsible, creative, and compassionate individuals who contribute positively to their community and the world.

Core Values
  • Excellence in teaching and learning
  • Respect and compassion for others
  • Integrity and responsibility
  • Service to the community
