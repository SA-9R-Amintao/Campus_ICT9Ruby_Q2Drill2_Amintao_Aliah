Enroll

Joining OBMC is simple. Follow the steps below and our admissions team will guide you through the process.

Requirements
  • Accomplished enrollment form
  • Original and photocopy of the student's birth certificate
  • Report card (Form 138) from the previous school
  • Certificate of good moral character
  • Two recent 2x2 ID photos
Enrollment Steps
  1. Submit the enrollment form and required documents to the registrar.
  2. Attend the student assessment or interview.
  3. Wait for the admission result and confirmation.
  4. Settle the enrollment fees at the cashier.
  5. Receive your class schedule and school orientation details.
